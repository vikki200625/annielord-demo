About

Interior design, made personal

I'm Annie, an interior designer creating homes that feel cosy, inviting and full of character. I specialise in traditional British style with a contemporary twist, blending timeless pieces with the way you actually live.

Whether you need a single room refreshed or a whole home designed, I offer flexible design packages to suit every space and budget, with remote consultations available across the UK.
